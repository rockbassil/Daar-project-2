Human relevance evaluation — uncompleted worksheets

Use anonymous IDs; participant slots are not evidence of recruited people. Allow approximately 20–30 minutes per participant; offer breaks. Participation is voluntary. Rankings are hidden on these sheets. Do not tell participants which algorithm produced a trial.

After the session, the facilitator transfers judgments to feedback.csv using manifest.json to map trial IDs to task/ranking. Put recommendation_rating and task_completed on rank 1 only. Do not fill blanks with invented results. Shared books between trials can cause learning effects.
